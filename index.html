Research statement

My research asks how businesses and consumers adopt, interpret, and use new technologies in marketing. I am particularly interested in generative AI, immersive technologies such as VR, AR, and the metaverse, and in how brands, especially luxury brands, build value in digital and virtual environments. My work is shaped by years of practical experience in international markets, which helps me connect academic questions to real managerial problems.

Previous research: thesis work

Master's thesis (Università degli Studi di Torino, July 2026): Generative AI in the Marketing Industry: The Ambivalence of a Disruptive Revolution. Supervised by Prof. Fabiana Vernero, with co-supervisor Prof. Maria Grazia Turri. The thesis asks in what ways the ambivalence of generative AI shows up in marketing, and what its strengths and risks are. It combines a survey of marketing professionals in Northern Italy with semi-structured interviews at a leading creative agency in Milan, to understand how AI is adopted and perceived in practice and how agencies handle the trade-off between efficiency and creativity. The findings point to a marked duality: the same capabilities that enable personalization and scale also carry risks of manipulation and creative homogenization.

Bachelor's thesis (Università degli Studi di Torino, July 2024): The Luxury Fashion Brands in the Metaverse. Supervised by Prof. Maria Grazia Turri. The thesis explores how luxury fashion brands use the metaverse, with technologies such as virtual reality, augmented reality, and blockchain, to create personalized and engaging consumer experiences. It examines the metaverse as a business model, including hyper-personalization, data, digital assets, and NFTs, and analyzes brand collaborations such as Balenciaga with Fortnite, Burberry with Blankos Block Party, Louis Vuitton's Louis The Game, and Prada with Adidas. It argues that luxury brands are not only following consumer trends but positioning themselves as innovators in digital commerce, with benefits including new revenue streams, cost savings, and greater creative freedom.
