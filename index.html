PhD Student in Marketing | College of Business and Analytics, Southern Illinois University Carbondale

Studying how AI, VR, AR, the metaverse, and other emerging technologies are reshaping marketing, brands, and consumer experience.

I am a PhD student in Marketing at Southern Illinois University Carbondale, a Carnegie R1 research university. I am passionate about new technologies and how they transform marketing. My research examines how artificial intelligence, virtual and augmented reality, the metaverse, and other emerging technologies are adopted, perceived, and used by brands and consumers, from luxury fashion in virtual worlds to generative AI in marketing practice.

Before starting my doctoral studies, I spent nearly two decades working in international export, logistics, and customer operations, which shapes my applied, industry-aware approach to research.
