About SIU Carbondale

Southern Illinois University Carbondale was officially designated an R1 institution, Very High Research Spending and Doctorate Production, in the Carnegie Classification in February 2025. Fewer than 5% of nearly 4,000 classified institutions hold this designation. In Illinois, SIU is one of only three public R1 universities and the only R1 in a rural area.
